Our services

Care for every stage of life

From everyday check-ups to specialist care, PrimeCare brings together experienced doctors across six departments — all bookable online.

General Practice

Routine check-ups, screenings, and everyday illness or injury care from a qualified GP. Ideal for ongoing health concerns, referrals, and general wellness visits for the whole family.

Cardiology

Comprehensive heart health assessments, including ECGs, blood pressure monitoring, and specialist consultations for patients managing or at risk of cardiovascular conditions.

Paediatrics

Gentle, thorough care for infants, children, and teens — from routine growth check-ups and vaccinations to diagnosing and treating childhood illnesses.

Neurology

Assessment and ongoing management of neurological conditions such as headaches, migraines, seizures, and nerve-related concerns, with referrals for further investigation where needed.

Preventive Care

Vaccinations, wellness plans and lifestyle counselling.

Women's Health

Gynaecological care, antenatal check-ups, and wellness screenings tailored to women at every life stage, delivered with privacy and care.

Basic Pathology & Screening

On-site blood pressure, glucose, and cholesterol screening for quick same-day insight into your health, with lab referrals available for more detailed testing.
